What Information Should Be Reported After Crypto Fraud?

How to organize incident details for exchanges, financial institutions, law enforcement, and other legitimate reporting channels.

Key points

Cryptocurrency fraud can move quickly. If you believe you have been targeted, stop sending additional funds and preserve the original records before deleting messages, accounts, or files.

What to document

  • Transaction hashes or IDs
  • Wallet addresses and blockchain/network
  • Dates and approximate amounts
  • Website URLs, usernames, and email addresses
  • Screenshots, chat messages, invoices, and payment records

Protect yourself

Do not provide anyone with your seed phrase, private key, password, or authentication code. Be especially cautious when someone promises guaranteed recovery or demands another payment to unlock or recover funds.

Next steps

Consider contacting the relevant cryptocurrency exchange or service, your financial institution when applicable, and appropriate law-enforcement or regulatory reporting channels. An investigation may help organize evidence and review transaction information, but cryptocurrency recovery is never guaranteed.

Educational information: This article is general educational information, not legal, financial, investment, or law-enforcement advice.
